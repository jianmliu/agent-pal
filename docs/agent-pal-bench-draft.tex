\begin{abstract}

Persistent role-playing games combine dialogue comprehension, spatial exploration, cross-scene causality, and resource management. We introduce \textbf{Agent-PAL-Bench}, a text-based agent benchmark built on a headless execution environment for \emph{The Legend of Sword and Fairy}. Agents issue structured tool calls and receive textual game-state feedback; gameplay video is not an agent input. Beyond the default \texttt{assist} (L1) layer that exposes high-level semantic tools, the environment implements a \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} mode that exposes only low-level protocol primitives (L0) and in-session dynamic self-coding (\texttt{run\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}code} and \texttt{module\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}reload}). The reviewed working catalog contains 23 tasks and 67 planned task--seed episodes. Tasks specify starting states, state-based goals, budgets, information conditions, and reference trajectories. A current-build audit reaches the goal on 59/67 reference trajectories, exposing release-blocking failures rather than model errors. A separate, frozen two-task audit repairs missing appearance cues and premature goal detection, calibrates action budgets, and evaluates three built-in cloud agents and one externally scaffolded agent on game seed 42. All eight evaluation episodes exhaust their action budgets without success and pass trajectory replay; calibration and reference successes are reported separately. In addition, we validate the \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} mode end-to-end under deterministic lockstep (\texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}seed\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}1}) across three external agents (Argon, GPT-6.1-Sol, and Claude Opus 5.5): operating strictly from \texttt{SMSG\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}*} state packets and \texttt{CMSG\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}*} primitives, the three agents independently synthesize 731-line, 135-line, and 1,427-line runtime libraries in session scope (spanning bytecode-graph BFS pathfinders, connected-component dungeon planners, resistance-adaptive combat engines, and menu state machines), each completing all 11 chapters from a new game to the final boss (\texttt{Baiyue}, 32,760 HP) across sequential save checkpoints (\texttt{Slots\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}1--99}). Furthermore, across four targeted behavioral and ablation studies on \textbf{Dali Rain Altar (\texttt{classic48\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}rain\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}orbs})}, \textbf{Baihe Village Medicine (\texttt{classic10\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}baihe\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}medicine})}, \textbf{Final Boss Baiyue (\texttt{baiyue\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}single\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}origin})}, and \textbf{Yangzhou Purple Gold Gourd (\texttt{classic19\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}yangzhou\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}gourd})}---paired with a 22-question closed-book prior quiz (\texttt{bench\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}quiz}), cross-seed replication, budget expansion, and a counterfactual no-cheese ablation stripping \texttt{Jiushen}, \texttt{Qian\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Kun\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Yi\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Zhi}, and \texttt{Yingu}---we evaluate 10--12 frontier and open-weight cloud models, uncovering: (1) how 45\ensuremath{^\circ} isometric chiral symmetry ambiguity triggers 100K-token deductive loops in reasoning LLMs and how 1-step epistemic probes and per-turn token circuit breakers resolve them; (2) how closed-book quizzes separate pretraining walkthrough contamination from genuine zero-shot room-by-room discovery and expose single-cutoff truncation illusions; (3) a two-layer prior structure (walkthrough meta priors versus domain mechanics priors), recency-biased overcorrection during in-context retry learning, and the delayed batch-execution credit-assignment trap (\texttt{\ensuremath{\Delta}HP\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x1\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x2\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x3}) versus controlled-variable linear elimination in turn-based combat; and (4) how partial false priors (\texttt{gourd\#q4} well-side misremembering) induce confirmation-bias search stalls (82 versus 22 calls) on a 15-scene detective chain, while a zero-prior agent recovers from post-victory loot omission and re-purposes its earlier four-rooftop chase topology (\texttt{Episodic\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Spatial\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Graph\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Reuse}) to reach an elevated observation point. A follow-up evaluation of GPT-6 Astra on game seed 42 reaches the Rain Altar, Baihe Medicine, and Baiyue goals in 13, 107, and 28 calls respectively, with an 11-round first-attempt boss victory; all three pass action replay and source-bound external-client accounting checks after an append-only accounting repair. A separate seed-42 Astra boss ablation removes Jiushen, Qian Kun Yi Zhi, and Yingu on load and reload; Astra wins on attempt 2 in 220 cumulative calls and 54 winning rounds, with full trajectory/accounting verification. A later seed-42 Yangzhou episode reaches the task goal in 146 calls under the original 230-call cap, compared with historical Argon at 161, Sol at 218, and Opus at 280 after continuation beyond its initial 230-call failure. These are individual episodes compared with historical Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, and Gemini 4 Argon records under different budgets and builds, rather than repeated matched trials. The findings identify observation fidelity, context retention, prior contamination, metacognitive stopping rules, tool-abstraction layer, and self-coded architectural divergence as measurement concerns. They do not establish a regional model advantage, a causal success-rate gain from the repairs, or a measure of planning alone.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Introduction}{Introduction}}

Knowing where to find the Water Spirit Orb is different from finding the relevant characters, retaining a necessary item, crossing scenes, and completing an exchange within an action budget. Knowing that a final boss requires damage is different from conserving resources through prerequisite fights and adapting when a healer is silenced or incapacitated. A persistent game lets us examine how an agent turns information into consequential actions.

We use a Chinese fantasy RPG because dialogue, character relationships, implicit prerequisites, map mechanisms, equipment, and consumables interact in one authored world. Whether familiarity with Chinese game language and culture improves situated decisions is an open research question. Model developer location does not measure that familiarity. Scale, pretraining, reasoning budget, tools, information access, and agent scaffolding must be controlled before attributing differences to cultural knowledge.

This paper contributes: (1) a working collection of replayable RPG segments, including same-goal starting-state variants, causal item chains, and synthetic combat configurations; (2) a protocol based on fixed saves, game seeds, virtual time, state predicates, and action replay; (3) explicit tool and hint conditions, distinguishing the built-in white-box high-level tool layer (\texttt{assist} L1) from the low-level protocol and dynamic self-coding layer (\texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} L0 + \texttt{run\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}code}/\texttt{module\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}reload}); (4) a two-task development audit showing how missing observations, an early-success predicate, and context policy complicate failure attribution, complemented by an end-to-end \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} self-coding playthrough from new game to final credits that validates L0 protocol completeness; and (5) four targeted cognitive-behavioral and counterfactual ablation studies across spatial symmetry puzzles (\texttt{classic48\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}rain\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}orbs}), multi-stage open exploration (\texttt{classic10\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}baihe\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}medicine}), high-pressure boss combat (\texttt{baiyue\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}single\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}origin}), and a 15-scene detective chain (\texttt{classic19\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}yangzhou\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}gourd}), quantifying deductive loop traps, zero-shot exploration overhead, partial false-prior belief updating, episodic spatial graph reuse, in-context retry evolution, and batch-execution credit assignment. A three-scenario GPT-6 Astra follow-up adds source-indexed, replay-verified episodes and an explicit comparison of calls, retries, and winning battle rounds (Section 5.12), followed by a source-audited boss ablation (Section 5.13) and a separately verified Yangzhou detective-chain episode (Section 5.14). The implemented environment, development validations, targeted behavioral slices, and the proposed full-catalog inferential study have different evidential status.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Related Work}{Related Work}}

\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688}{AgentBench} evaluates agents across interactive environments. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.11532}{TextWorld} supports state tracking and controlled generation of text games; our tasks use an existing authored RPG. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05398}{Jericho} studies human-authored interactive fiction. Agent-PAL-Bench shares its exploration demands and also exposes party, equipment, consumable, and combat systems. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07540}{ScienceWorld} distinguishes factual recall from applying knowledge in an interactive environment. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03768}{ALFWorld} connects text and embodied environments, highlighting the distinction between text-mediated decisions and visual understanding. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06613}{GameBench} and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13543}{BALROG} provide broader game-based comparisons.

RPG agency itself is not new: the \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15563}{PokeAgent Challenge} includes competitive battling and RPG speedrunning, and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08340}{PokeGym} evaluates visually driven long-horizon tasks and adaptation. Our scope is a Chinese-language authored RPG with inspectable task predicates and structured text interaction. We make no priority claim for RPG planning, persistent worlds, or game-agent evaluation. Our reported scores cannot be compared directly to visual or differently scaffolded benchmarks.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Environment and Tasks}{Environment and Tasks}}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Interaction and replay}{Interaction and replay}}

Each episode runs an isolated game process from a new game, a classic RPG save, or a fixed benchmark save slot, with a game random seed. Tools include \texttt{observe}, \texttt{map}, movement, interaction, dialogue advance, menu choices, items, spells, and battle actions. Game simulation is paused between calls; advancing actions move virtual time, whereas observation and reference-material queries do not. Model inference latency therefore does not itself advance the game. Wall-time limits remain a separate harness constraint. Saves and map memory are isolated between episodes. Video is for human inspection, not the B/C-tier agent or grader. The reviewed task catalog opens no visual A-tier tasks.

A task defines its start, goal predicates, optional milestones and failure conditions, budgets, and tools. The grader reads state such as scene visits, inventory, event-object state, battle outcomes, and dialogue history. \texttt{check} replays references. \texttt{verify} replays logged actions and compares per-step grading state and terminal game outcomes. It does not rerun model inference or independently validate token accounting, cost, or model-side stopping causes. For new external CLI runs, an append-only \texttt{client\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}finalized} event binds the original server-end log prefix and raw client output by SHA-256. The verifier checks reported usage against that output separately from game replay; missing or interrupted usage is unknown rather than zero. This is consistency checking, not independent provider billing or backend-model attestation. Standard replay is not a proof that all observation text was complete or accurate; the appearance audit adds native observation checks and selected response-equivalence checks. A successful reference is a feasibility witness for that version and seed, not an optimal policy or a model result.

The current general-task runner records a Git revision label, task versions, seeds, prompt version, and logs; battle-scored runs additionally record runtime/asset and task/save fingerprints. General-task runs do not automatically receive all those hashes. The frozen appearance experiment therefore supplies separate manifests. A \texttt{-\allowbreak{}dirty} label alone cannot identify the exact executable, assets, or task contents.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Information and context conditions}{Information and context conditions}}

B provides structured text tools; C adds walkthrough and atlas access. Independently, \texttt{standard} suppresses some script-derived purpose/requirement hints that \texttt{full} exposes. Standard is not a guarantee of pixel-equivalent information: structured coordinates, IDs, navigation categories, and state summaries remain abstractions. Item-effect information may still be present under standard. \texttt{observe(\allowbreak{}full\allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{}true)} requests expanded observation; it is distinct from the environment's \texttt{hints\allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{}full} setting.

The built-in loop retains the full text of only the latest \textbf{six tool results}, replacing older results with placeholders while preserving assistant text and call records. An earlier clue may disappear unless the agent records or reacquires it. Claude CLI manages its own context. Built-in usage is checked against the token budget after each model turn; external CLI usage is merged only at episode end, so the task token cap is not enforced incrementally there. These differences require separate reporting. Translating only the brief does not remove Chinese game dialogue; an English-only observation condition and comprehensive name masking are not implemented.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Metrics and Proposed Experimental Protocol}{Metrics and Proposed Experimental Protocol}}

The implemented primary score is task success averaged across recorded seeds and then equally across tasks with results. \texttt{complete} indicates whether planned episodes are present; incomplete runs must not enter a formal ranking. For runs consisting entirely of Baiyue battle-scored tasks, ties in success are broken by \textbf{mean battle rounds among wins}, lower first. Ordinary runs instead use mean monetary cost among successes; mixed scoring has no meaningful common efficiency tie-break. Tool calls are not combat rounds. No-success efficiency statistics are undefined, not zero.

Diagnostic metrics include milestone progress, median calls to success, invalid-call rate, virtual time, tokens, and recorded cost. Invalid rate is total invalid calls divided by total tool calls, not an average of episode percentages. Expected movement/interaction interruptions caused by battle or dialogue are soft stops and are excluded from invalid calls. Failure progress counts achieved milestones divided by one plus the number of milestones; success sets it to one. Neither progress nor reference-relative call efficiency is an independent capability score. Zero recorded local/cloud cost can mean unpriced usage, not zero computation cost.

Freeze executable/build, assets, saves, tasks/references, goals, prompt, observation/context policy, model identity, decoding/reasoning settings, information sources, call/token/virtual-time/wall limits, and exclusions. Planned formal comparisons use at least three game seeds and three independent decoding samples per condition; this is a minimum grid, not a power guarantee. A fixed game seed does not fix cloud sampling. Publish per-episode outcomes, paired task/family differences, and uncertainty clustered by related task family. Many more independent families are needed for reliable group-level inference. Externally scaffolded agents remain separate; developer origin cannot identify a cultural effect.

Every included task--seed pair must pass reference replay on the frozen build. Every recorded episode must be checked, but a matching replay alone does not convert a provider/harness failure into a valid model failure. Prespecify handling of infrastructure faults, stopping causes, and missing episodes. Public references and development traces create contamination risks; a private test split and independent audit remain future work.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Development Audits and Exploratory Results}{Development Audits and Exploratory Results}}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Main working-tree reference audit}{Main working-tree reference audit}}

On 16 October 2026, the reviewed 23-task working tree passed schema validation and no-engine tests. Full \texttt{check\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}jobs\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}2} reached the goal on \textbf{59/67 reference trajectories}. Five Baiyue failures requested Qian Kun Yi Zhi from a member without that spell at the observed command window: fragile-stocked seed 42, mana-budget seed 43, origin seed 43, and sustain-no-wine seeds 42/43. Three tower references missed the target: tower-chain seeds 43/44 and tower-gate seed 43. These are reference failures, not model scores; their root causes and repairs remain open. Historical Baiyue reference successes on older builds do not override this current-build result. The one-seed palace reserve reference passed, but remains a development pilot.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Local-model pipeline pilot and historical evidence}{Local-model pipeline pilot and historical evidence}}

Qwen2.5-3B (\texttt{ollama\allowbreak{}:\allowbreak{}qwen2\allowbreak{}.\allowbreak{}5\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}bench\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}32k\allowbreak{}:\allowbreak{}3b}), built-in B-standard, ran the reference-passing handkerchief task on seeds 42/43/44, prompt version 3, code label \texttt{3e316b4c\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}dirty}, and a 120-second wall limit. It completed \textbf{0/3}, made partial progress on two seeds, and ended \texttt{agent\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}stopped} on all three after repeated text-only turns. Calls/invalid calls were 8/3, 4/3, and 6/3. All three trajectories passed replay. Its five-question closed-book quiz scored \textbf{0/5}. This validates two measurement paths on one model/task; it does not identify whether knowledge or tool use caused failure.

A Qwen2.5-14B seed-42 attempt was killed at a 180-second wall limit and labeled \texttt{harness\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}crash\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(exit\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}null)}. A partial seed-43 trace also exists after the interrupted scheduling attempt; there is no complete result for it, and seed 44 has no completed result. The attempted run is excluded from model denominators. The fallback's zero calls are not a measurement of the actual partial trajectory. The timeout label requires repair before formal evaluation.

Older repository notes report external Claude Sonnet 9/9 versus local Qwen 0/9 on a three-task pilot, B 0/3 versus C 1/3 on a first-meeting pilot, and 2/3 on a one-life island variant. These are historical, differently versioned records, not additional results in the present denominator or causal ablations. The reported GPT-6 Astra playthrough with roughly 22 hours of footage allowed walkthrough use, rollback, and human strategy discussion; it is development context rather than an independently scored benchmark episode. Model identity and the modifier-free claim require an archived playthrough manifest before use as research evidence.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Medicine and monkey observation/goal audit}{Medicine and monkey observation/goal audit}}

The two audited tasks are \texttt{classic10\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}baihe\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}medicine} and \texttt{classic31\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}banana\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}monkeys}. The original B observation reduced some plants and animals to generic navigation labels, omitting cues visible in the rendered game. The audit first forwarded \texttt{observe(\allowbreak{}full\allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{}true)} correctly so expanded inventory/state could be requested, and corrected item feedback that suggested interaction while dialogue was still open. The monkey goal previously accepted an opening banana-offering line before passage was actually clear. The revised goal requires event object 3482 in scene 192 to reach state 0; the reference now takes 23 calls rather than the prematurely accepted 22.

A subsequent appearance repair shares a reviewed catalog between local \texttt{observe} and scene-wide \texttt{map}. It matches MGO sprite bytes by SHA-256 and MAP/GOP background pairs by their hashes. Coverage is \textbf{64 sprite resources and nine map/background pairs} from both reference routes, including non-goal objects. Captions describe broad leaves and hanging yellow curved fruit, a small tree with reddish-orange round fruit, a water bank, a brown antlered deer, and a circular toothed metal object. They do not name a quest purpose, required offering, reward, or action order. Briefs were unchanged. This supplies visible evidence without explicitly stating the solution.

The catalog is a manually reviewed static/first-frame abstraction, not a runtime vision model or all-game coverage. Unknown or replaced content falls back to neutral descriptions; the lookup assumes immutable assets within a process. To examine the metacognitive stopping rules of reasoning LLMs in interactive state machines, we evaluated 12 model and reasoning configurations on \texttt{classic48\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}rain\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}orbs} (placing the Five Spirit Orbs on the Dali Rain Altar, reference \texttt{par\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}7}, action budget \texttt{80} calls, \texttt{Tier\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}B}, \texttt{standard} hints, \texttt{seed\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}42}). After the agent embeds the Holy Spirit Orb (\texttt{Shengling\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}Zhu}) into the stone tablet (\texttt{id\allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{}4923}), the tablet inscription specifies orthogonal compass associations (West = Wind, East = Thunder, South = Earth, North = Fire, Center = Water), whereas the five raised altar pillars form a diagonal quincunx on the 45\ensuremath{^\circ} isometric grid: center pillar \texttt{4929\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(101\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}94)} is surrounded by \texttt{4926\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(97\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}90)}, \texttt{4927\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(105\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}90)}, \texttt{4925\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(97\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}98)}, and \texttt{4928\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}(105\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}98)} at offsets \texttt{(\ensuremath{\pm}4\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\pm}4)}. \section{\texorpdfstring{Discussion and Limitations}{Discussion and Limitations}}

\textbf{Construct and information validity.} Correct tool syntax, available clues, retained clues, recalled game knowledge, tool self-coding, and successful planning are different observations. Missing visual semantics and premature goal detection were environment defects. Repairs improve the measurement contract; they do not warrant attributing every later failure to the model. A stronger study needs observation/context ablations and independent failure annotation. The targeted studies in Sections 5.8--5.11 further demonstrate that: (1) when an environment exhibits chiral symmetry ambiguity or unobservable state, reasoning models lacking a metacognitive stopping rule can fall into 100K-token deductive loops, whereas a 1-step epistemic probe or an exogenous per-turn token circuit breaker resolves the ambiguity immediately; (2) in canonical game benchmarks, pretraining priors operate at two layers (surface walkthrough memorization and deep RPG mechanics priors), and partial or locally distorted surface priors (such as \texttt{gourd\#q4}'s false well-side memory) can induce confirmation bias and 80-step search stalls, requiring closed-book quizzes (\texttt{bench\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}quiz}) alongside counterfactual mechanism ablations (\texttt{BENCH\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}STRIP\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}CHEESE\allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{}1}) to disentangle memorized speedruns, false-prior belief updating, and zero-shot scientific induction or episodic spatial graph reuse; and (3) under delayed batch execution (\texttt{\ensuremath{\Delta}HP\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}round\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}=\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x1\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x2\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}x3}), modeling the measurement instrument's temporal semantics and performing controlled-variable elimination is essential to avoid credit-assignment illusions. While \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} mode removes white-box L1 pre-checks and static heuristic limits, it couples code synthesis and debugging skill with domain planning, requiring the paired E8 protocol for separation.

\textbf{Sampling and adaptation.} The four original-resource Astra episodes (Sections 5.12 and 5.14) and one separate ablation episode (Section 5.13) each use only seed 42 and are compared with differently versioned historical records; they do not expand the original ten-model boss denominator or support an additional population success-rate estimate. Calibration uses one external agent and two seeds. The exploratory evaluation in Section 5.5 uses one sample per task/model on a seed already used in earlier development comparisons; the token correction further uses evaluation feedback. While the diagnostic studies in Sections 5.8--5.11 add cross-seed replication (\texttt{seed\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}43}), multi-tier budget expansion (\texttt{120\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}240\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}320\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}\ensuremath{\rightarrow}\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}560\allowbreak{}/\allowbreak{}700}), and in-context retry loops, they focus on four representative tasks. Selected difficult tasks and correlated seeds cannot unbiasedly estimate overall RPG competence, and no cultural or regional effect is identified. Synthetic saves and modified classic-save levels/cash also limit natural-play interpretations.

\textbf{Scaffolding and budgets.} CLI and built-in agents differ in context retention, token enforcement, and usage reporting. Equal call limits do not imply equal compute. Section 5.9 shows that a single hard call cap (\texttt{120} calls) induces a truncation illusion that conflates thorough zero-shot village exploration (100\% completion in 143--176 calls) with genuine stagnation, while Section 5.8 shows that the per-turn output token ceiling (\texttt{4\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{}096} versus \texttt{16\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{}384}) directly alters reasoning-model trajectories. Under \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} mode, a single \texttt{run\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}code} invocation can dispatch dozens of underlying \texttt{CMSG\allowbreak{}\_\allowbreak{}*} commands inside the sandbox; comparisons against \texttt{assist} mode must therefore report both top-level MCP tool calls and underlying L0 command counts (\texttt{commands\allowbreak{}Sent}). Costs for cloud/local adapters are not consistently priced. Failed runs have no winning-round or success-efficiency statistic. The three-task 9/9 reference check in Section 5.12 does not supersede Section 5.1's historical 59/67 catalog audit. Full-catalog reference status and wall-time classification still require a frozen re-audit.

\textbf{Reproduction and release.} A replay establishes action/outcome consistency in its recorded environment, not reproducibility of cloud generation or validity across engine updates. General tasks need explicit manifests beyond Git labels. The accompanying evidence indexes contain extracted numbers, provenance, and hashes. Original-resource and ablation Astra traces, their source/hash indexes, and preserved pre-repair evidence are included in the repository. Matching execution snapshots remain local, so trace availability does not imply independently reproducible build or runtime availability. Proprietary assets and music are not proposed for redistribution; asset rights and release permissions need review. Public references increase contamination risk, and a hidden test set is not yet implemented.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Conclusion}{Conclusion}}

Agent-PAL-Bench provides a working protocol and task collection for text-mediated decisions in a Chinese RPG, supporting both a built-in high-level semantic tool layer (\texttt{assist} L1) and a low-level protocol plus dynamic self-coding layer (\texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} L0). The current evidence supports partial reference feasibility, a local end-to-end pilot, a documented two-task observation/goal audit, and a three-agent end-to-end \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} completeness validation in which Argon, GPT-6.1-Sol, and Claude Opus 5.5 independently synthesized 731-line, 135-line, and 1,427-line runtime libraries to complete the entire game from new game to final credits (\texttt{Slots\allowbreak{} \allowbreak{}1--99}). Complementary multi-model behavioral and counterfactual ablation studies on the Dali Rain Altar (\texttt{classic48\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}rain\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}orbs}), Baihe Village Medicine (\texttt{classic10\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}baihe\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}medicine}), Final Boss Baiyue (\texttt{baiyue\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}single\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}origin}), and the Yangzhou Purple Gold Gourd detective chain (\texttt{classic19\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}yangzhou\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}gourd}) demonstrate that effective agency depends not only on static reasoning depth, but on issuing 1-step epistemic probes when pure deduction hits symmetry limits, balancing exhaustive zero-shot exploration against fast-pivoting elasticity, rapidly falsifying locally distorted pretraining priors while re-purposing cross-scene episodic spatial graphs, accumulating invariants monotonically across retries rather than overcorrecting to recent failures, and applying controlled-variable elimination to untangle delayed batch-execution feedback. The Astra follow-ups reach four original-resource task goals, including Yangzhou in 146 calls and a first-attempt 11-round boss victory, with gameplay and source-bound accounting verified; the separate no-cheese episode wins on attempt 2 with 220 calls and 54 winning rounds. These single-seed historical comparisons remain exploratory. A frozen release, repaired references, controlled scaffolding, independent samples, and a held-out evaluation remain necessary before comparative capability, tool-layer, or cultural-knowledge conclusions.

\item Integrate/version the isolated observation repairs and add complete manifests for general-task runs; do not silently substitute runtimes.
\item Compare capability-matched models under controlled context and enforceable budgets (including E1--E7 and paired \texttt{assist} versus \texttt{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}pure} E8 trials); separate CLI results, report both top-level calls and underlying L0 command counts, and report task-family uncertainty.
\item Expand natural resource chains across seeds; label synthetic variants and single-seed pilots.
\item Implement independent samples, information/context/language controls and a genuinely held-out split; preregister analysis before test access.
\item Archive allowed runtime/trace evidence, audit failure labels and save provenance, and prepare rights/license documentation.

\end{itemize}
